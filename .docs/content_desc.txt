"One day the great European War will come out of some damned foolish thing in the Balkans" — Otto von Bismark, 1888

Ever noticed that despite being called "The Powder Keg of Europe", nothing really happens in the Balkans most games?

That's what lead led me to create what's now Gates of the Bosphorus (formerly Greece, Byzantium, & the Balkans Flavor). This mod aims to add historical and pseudo-historical flavor to the nations of the Balkans and Anatolia, along with some alt-history content for Byzantium. The Balkans during this time frame were a mess both internally and externally and this mod strives to bring this struggle to live in Victoria 3. This mod adds several hundred events, dozens of journal entries, decisions, new monuments, and several historical characters to Greece and several Balkan states along with new event chains, dynamic names, and journal entries for Byzantium.

NOTE: This mod will not provide any DLC-exclusive content or integrations unless you own the respective DLC!

Content for Greece:
• The Regency Council: A short event chain showcasing Armansperg's interference in 1836
• The Bavarocracy: Guide Greece through the "Bavarocracy" under King Otto up to the election of George I; integrates with the "Bavarocracy" journal from IP3
• The Dedilomeni Principle: Establish legitimacy in the democratic process under George I
• The Military League: Experience a new series of events and journals through the Military League and the Rise of Venizelos
• The Megali Idea: Enforce the Greek state's claims over the Aegean; integrates with the base game "Greek Nationalism" journal
• The Revenge of the Greeks: Gain claims on and spread your influence over Alexander's former empire
• Greek Nationalism: Establish a discrete Greek identity as a Hellenic, Roman, or Hybrid society
• Greek Cultural Roots: Conduct a series of excavations for the Greek Archaeological Service
• The Language Question: Enforce a national dialect on the Greek state
• The Women's Journal: Support the efforts of Kalliroi Parren in gaining basic rights for women
• Custom Corinth Canal and the Old Royal Palace buildings

Content for Greater Greece:
• The Xenocracy: Develop the islands into a "model colony" within the British Empire
• The Jewry of the Ionian Islands: Influence the role of the Jews of the Ionians
• Samos in Submission: Navigate the authoritarian rule of Stefanos Bogoridi and assert the autonomy of the Principality of Samos from the Porte
• Greek Identity: Two new cultures "Rhomaios" and "Urum" to represent the fractured Greek world of the 1800s.

Content for Byzantium:
• Greek Nationalism: Start as a Rhomaios country and assert their identity against the tide of Hellenism
• The Purple Pheonix: Events and journal entries to reclaim the former Roman Empire
• Ability to rebuild the Imperial Palace and the Hippodrome, along with a decision to revive chariot racing
• Cappadocia, Trebizond, & Theodoro as playable starting tags
• Dynamic country names for Byzantium and a game rule to choose between "Byzantine" or "Roman" Empire names

Content for Montenegro:
• The Prince-Bishropic: Guide Montenegro into becoming a modern nation state; integrates with the "From Tribes to Nation" journal from IP3
• The Black Moutain: Stand your ground against neighboring Ottoman Pashas as they attempt to assert Ottoman authority over Montenegro; integrates with the "Raiding" journal from IP3
• The Wrath of Ali Pasha: Deal with the antics of the upstart Ottoman noble, Ali Pasha of Bosnia
• Custom buildings for the Biljarda and Montenegrin Mountain Roadways

Content for Serbia:
• Serbian Monarchism: Experience the back-and-forth between houses Obrenović and Karađorđević for control over Serbia; integrates with the "Throne of Thorns" journal from IP3
• Načertanije: Take charge of the Yugoslavist movement and establish Serbia as the pre-eminent Yugoslav state; integrates with the "Forming Yugoslavia" journal from IP3
• Serbian Nationalism: Reject Pan-Nationalism to reform the Serbian Empire

Content for Bulgaria:
• The Unification of Bulgaria: Seek Unification with Eastern Rumelia as Bulgaria and break free from Ottoman suzerainty after the Great Eastern Crisis
• The Battenburg Monarchy: Struggle to maintain power as Bulgarian King Alexander I
• The Golden Lion Roars: Assert Bulgarian revanchism to restore either the Bulgarian Empire or the Byzantine Empire
• The Revival of Bulgaria: Play as the Eyalet of Silistra and seek autonomy from the Ottoman Empire
• Integrates with the "Prussia of the Balkans" journal from IP3

Content for Romania:
• Regulamentul Organic: Guide the Romanian Principalities through their transition from post-Ottoman state & elect a new Hospodar every 7 years
• Romanian Liberalism: Ride the wave of the Revolutions of 1848 in the Principalities
• Romanian Unification: Unify the Romanian Principalities and provoke radicalism in Transylvania.
• Romanian Nationalism: Tilt the Romanian state between Junimism and Dacianism with custom ideologies for leaders and agitators
• A Romanian Empire: Restore the Byzantine Empire as Romania with a fixed path to the Purple Phoenix
• Custom Peleș Castle building

Content for Bosnia:
• Start the game as Bosnia in the form of the Eyalet of Hersek
• The Ambitions of Ali Pasha: Fill the shoes of Ali Pasha Rizvanbegović as he attempts to crown himself as the "Sultan in Mostar"

Content for the Ottoman Empire:
• Complete overhaul of all Ottoman Content to better represent the prolonged last stand of the Empire
• The Sick Man of Europe: Reclaim the Ottoman Empire's position as the master of the near East by bringing its territories back under direct control.
• The Reorganization of the State: Lead the fight to regenerate the state apparatus of the Empire and throw your weight behind either the Ottomanist or Islamist movements
• The Eastern Question: Events and Mechanics surrounding the Eastern Question, with the Ottoman Empire facing persistent revolts across the Balkans modeled after the struggle system from CK3 and using the Situations Framework from CMF
• The Young Ottomans: Hold back or encourage the Reformist plot to unseat Sultan Abdulaziz
• The Black Mountain: Respond to and retaliate against Montenegrin raiders in the western fringes of the Empire
• The Pirates of the Adriatic: Deploy expeditions into the Adriatic to hunt down and exterminate Rafil Bey and his priate bands
• Custom buildings for the Topkapı and Dolmabahçe palaces

Content for Libya:
• Entrenching the State: Take charge of the Ottoman enforces to establish a permanent state presence in the Eyalet of Tripolitania
• The Teachings of al-Sanusi: Navigate the arrival of Muḥammad al-Sanūsī and the spread of the Sanūssiyya movement
• Securing the Desert: Lead expeditions to quell the Bedouins of the Libyan Deserts

Content for Tunis:
• The Modernization of Tunis: Lead Ahmad Mustafa's efforts to modernize the Tunisian government
• Visit the French court to seek legitimacy and build the al-Muhammadiya Palace
• Guard the border against infiltration by agents of the Sanūssiyya movement as it overtakes Libya

Content for Kurdistan:
• The Tribes of Kurdistan: Take charge of the Emirate of Soran in its doomed offensives under Mirê Kor
• The Unification of Kurdistan: Unite the tribes of Kurdistan under a singular ruler and declare yourself the Mir-i Miran
• Organize the Eyalet of Kurdistan to consolidate control from within the Ottoman Empire

Content for the Balkans at Large:
Events and Mechanics surrounding the Eastern Question, with the Ottoman Empire facing persistent revolts across the Balkans and scripting to simulate the Great Eastern Crisis of 1875-1878.
Eastern Orthodox Flavor, showcasing the pervasive role of the Orthodox Church in the Balkans.
Yugoslavism: Foster unity between the Slavic peoples of the Balkans to form Yugoslavia.
The Triune Dream: Minor journal entry for Slovenia and Croatia to form the Triune Kingdom.
